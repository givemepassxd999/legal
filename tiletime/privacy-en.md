# TileTime Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 2, 2026**
**Effective date: May 2, 2026**

TileTime (the "App") is independently developed and maintained. This policy describes how the App handles your data. Please read it carefully before using the App.

---

## 1. What data do we collect?

**The App does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information about you.**

All data the App needs to function is **stored only on your device**, including but not limited to:

- Audio cues you record
- Colors and images you select
- Your layout configuration (Full-screen / Dual) and theme preference

This data is never sent anywhere outside the App and is never shared with any third party.

## 2. Microphone permission

The App requests microphone permission **only to record audio cues that you actively initiate**.

- Recordings are stored only in the App's sandbox on your device
- Recordings are never uploaded, never sent to the cloud, never shared with third parties
- You may revoke microphone permission at any time in your device settings

## 3. Photo library permission

The App requests photo library access **only to let you choose images as visual cues**.

- Selected images are used only for the current configuration and are never sent anywhere
- The App does not scan, index, or read any image you have not actively selected

## 4. Third-party services

### Android version
The Android version uses **Google Firebase Analytics** only to collect **anonymous crash and stability data** to detect and fix bugs and improve app quality. Specifically, it collects:

- Crash events (with non-personal error summaries)
- App version, OS version, device model
- Country and language settings
- Built-in technical events such as app open and session start

**The app explicitly disables the following Firebase defaults:**

- ❌ Android Advertising ID (AAID / SSAID) collection
- ❌ Ad personalization signals
- ❌ Ad user data transmission
- ❌ Google Signals cross-device demographic inference

Firebase does not collect your name, email, or any other personally identifiable information, and the data is not used for advertising.

For details, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### iOS version
The iOS version uses **no third-party analytics, tracking, or crash reporting services**. All your data stays on the device.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

- All your data stays on your device
- When you uninstall the App, all related data (recordings, settings) is automatically removed by the operating system
- The App has no cloud sync; there is no separate account to delete

## 6. Children's privacy

The App is designed to be used by parents together with children, but **does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13**, in line with the principles of COPPA (US) and GDPR (EU) regarding children's data.

Because all recordings and images are configured locally by parents and are never transmitted to any server, the App never has access to any identifying data about children.

## 7. Data security

Although the App does not transmit data, the security of data on your device depends on:

- Your device lock / biometric authentication
- The update status of your operating system
- Whether you let others use your device

Please keep your device secure to protect your data.

## 8. Policy changes

This policy may be updated as App functionality evolves. For significant changes, you will be notified via the App's update notes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version.
